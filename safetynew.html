Safety Tips

🔒 Password Security

  • Use unique passwords for each account
  • Enable two-factor authentication (2FA)
  • Use a password manager
  • Never share passwords via email or text

📧 Email Safety

  • Verify sender's email address carefully
  • Don't click suspicious links
  • Be wary of urgent requests for information
  • Report phishing attempts to IT

🌐 Web Browsing

  • Look for HTTPS in website URLs
  • Keep your browser updated
  • Use ad-blockers and anti-tracking extensions
  • Avoid public Wi-Fi for sensitive transactions

📱 Mobile Security

  • Install apps only from official stores
  • Review app permissions carefully
  • Enable device encryption and lock screen
  • Keep your OS and apps updated
